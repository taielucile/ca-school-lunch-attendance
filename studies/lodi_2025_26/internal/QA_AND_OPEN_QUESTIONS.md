## Limits and unresolved source definitions

- **Attendance exposure.** The model uses 180 days and school Census enrollment as an annual enrollment stock, then proportionally allocates combined TK/K absences to K. Neither expected attendance days nor actual K attended days are available at the school level in these inputs.
- **Population mismatch.** CDE's absenteeism file is organized around *eligible cumulative enrollment*, excluding some short-duration/nonattending/exempt students; its absence measure and the Census enrollment snapshot are not necessarily the same population. Across the 31 matched schools, TK/K eligible cumulative enrollment totals **2,794**, versus **2,616** TK/K Census enrollment (difference **178**, with eligible cumulative higher at all 31 schools). See [CDE file definitions](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/ad/fsabr.asp). The size and direction of any resulting error in this K proxy cannot be determined from these files.
- **Timing.** The original meal workbook does not print a service-date range. Its ADP calculations are consistent with a 180-day divisor, but do not independently establish the covered service dates. The [2025–26 district calendar](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1736443433/lodiusdnet/fsjjkrvfjsh0bwpqtvlh/LodiUSD_InstructionalCalendar_2025-26.pdf) is July 29, 2025–May 29, 2026 for the modified traditional schedule; a school-specific calendar check may matter for other sites.
- **Grades.** Kindergarten meals are explicitly labeled in the workbook. `Preschool` is a separate category, not established as TK. The workbook's schoolwide Enrollment and Average Daily Attendance fields cannot validate K attendance.
- **Eligibility report discrepancy.** The independent kindergarten eligibility extract's `Highest Eligible` and `Last Day` columns each total **3,481**. They exceed K Census enrollment (1,763) at all 31 schools, by ratios ranging from 1.30 to 3.18. Their definitions and grade scope remain unresolved; they are not used in the model.
- **Small-school sensitivity.** Valley Robotics Academy has 12 K Census pupils, TK Census zero, and 13 reported TK/K absent days; its low lunch ratio is computed correctly but sensitive to the attendance-exposure assumptions. This is a review flag, not proof of a source error.
- **Measure meaning.** A meal count is a count of meals, not distinct pupils. Ratios per modeled student-day are not validated meal-participation percentages. These results support description, not causal explanations of school differences.

## November 11, 2026 — public-source follow-up on period and grade definitions

**Eligibility dates established; meal-workbook dates not established.** The team's `R/09_lodi_enrollment_triangulation.R` and source reconstruction confirm that the separate kindergarten-filtered eligibility report specifies **July 29, 2025–May 29, 2026**. The independent `Lodi Data_copy.xlsx` meal-count workbook has no explicit reporting dates in its inspected sheets. Its kindergarten breakfast and lunch ADP entries are consistent with dividing those grade meal totals by **180**, but that arithmetic cannot establish the actual source service window or the number of operating days at each site. Do not describe the two workbook periods as independently verified to match.

**Calendar counterexample.** [Lodi's 2025–26 modified traditional calendar](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1736443433/lodiusdnet/fsjjkrvfjsh0bwpqtvlh/LodiUSD_InstructionalCalendar_2025-26.pdf) specifies **July 29, 2025–May 29, 2026**. The [2025–26 Joe Serna Jr. Charter calendar](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1734135181/lodiusdnet/wjvi4e8wzirjyl9v9qky/Serna_InstructionalCalendar_2025-26.pdf) specifies **July 28, 2025–May 29, 2026**. Joe Serna is in the matched 31-school sample. Thus one common start date across all 31 schools is not independently supported by the public calendars. Calendars are not proof of meal-report operating days.

**Grade classification.** [Lodi's TK enrollment materials](https://www.lodiusd.net/enrollment/transitional-kindergarten) and [district TK policy](https://www.lodiusd.net/boardpolicies/6000/6170-1) treat TK as a K–12 pathway distinct from preschool enrollment. [California's Universal Meals FAQ](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ls/nu/univmealsfaq.asp) permits certain meal-pattern arrangements when preschool, TK and elementary students are served together. Neither source defines how the meal workbook's `Preschool` grade field was coded. The workbook's `Kindergarten` values remain identifiable by label, but the location of TK transactions is **not verified**. Do not infer that a source field called Preschool either contains or excludes TK without the report's grade codebook.

**Meal count definition.** The district's [Meals page](https://www.lodiusd.net/families/meals) states that students are offered free breakfast and lunch each school day, with lunch also served on minimum days. This is an **availability policy**, not proof of who received meals or of the specific workbook's inclusion rules. [7 CFR 210.2](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/7/210.2) defines average daily participation using meals claimed per operating day in federal lunch reporting; the Lodi workbook's ADP structure is consistent with that general convention, but the original meal report's transaction category, refund/adjustment conventions, reimbursable status and school-specific operating-day counts remain **unconfirmed**. Keep describing source `Total` as *kindergarten-labeled reported meal counts*, not verified distinct recipients or verified reimbursable meals.

**Implication for Angela's handoff:** Existing 31-school counts and modeled ratios do not change. The remaining verification question is narrow: obtain the original meal-report header/filters or district explanation specifying service dates, meal transaction criteria, grade-code mapping for TK and operating days. In their absence, present descriptive counts and label the attendance-denominator ratios as exploratory estimates, not validated participation rates.
