# School Meal Service and Kindergarten Schedules in California

**Proposal abstract (draft)**

California’s Universal Meals Program requires public schools to offer free breakfast and lunch, including to students in transitional kindergarten (TK) and kindergarten. But younger students may leave school before lunch is served, and schools vary in their meal schedules. This project will examine how school meal service varies and what existing records can show about its relationship to kindergarten schedules.

We will analyze California Department of Education meal claims for 2024–25 and compare them with available information about school schedules. The claims report meals by site and month, without grade-specific counts or attendance denominators, so they cannot establish TK or kindergarten meal service rates. We will also use preliminary records from three Irvine Unified schools for November 3–7, 2025. Vista Verde and Woodbury served approximately 11 and 9 kindergarten lunches per 100 attended student-days, compared with 46 and 48 in first grade. At Bonita Canyon, the rates were 45 and 51. All three schools were classified as having part-day kindergarten in 2024–25, but their kindergarten lunch rates differed.

The project will assess how far existing school and meal records can take an analysis of kindergarten meal service, and identify what additional data are needed to study schedule-related differences across schools.
