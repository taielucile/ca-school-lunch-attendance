# Focus-school calendar and program audit

This audit distinguishes calendar benchmarks from student-specific expected attendance exposure. A school's instructional calendar does not show when each student enrolled, withdrew, or was expected to attend.

The Aspire calendars show why a general Aspire day-count claim should not replace school-specific evidence. Vincent Shalvey's calendar does not print a total; its 184-weekday count is manually derived from the dates and no-school entries. River Oaks prints its totals directly.

## Calendar sensitivity

The primary Irvine/Lodi comparison remains the common 180-day benchmark. It is not an observed attendance denominator. A supplementary Lodi calculation using River Oaks' 176 calendar days and Vincent Shalvey's 184 weekdays, while leaving all other schools at 180, changes the cumulative-versus-Census proxy gap from 7.98599% to approximately 7.98513%. This calculation treats each Census or cumulative enrollee as exposed for every counted school day; it does not solve the student-entry/exit problem and is not used to select a preferred denominator.

## Rio Valley and the meal-access sample

Rio Valley remains in the reproducible school universe. For an estimand about meals served at a physical school site, its independent-study/home-school model is not equivalent to a campus-based school meal opportunity. The primary campus-based sample should exclude it unless the source identifies where meals are served and which TK/K students are included. Preserve a separate Rio Valley sensitivity/stratum rather than silently deleting its denominator row.

## Sources and interpretation

- [CDE Instructional Time FAQ](https://www.cde.ca.gov/fg/it/instructionaltimefaq.asp): charter schools must be offered at least 175 instructional days; independent-study pupils are subject to annual day and minute requirements.
- [IUSD Pre-Kindergarten page](https://iusd.org/about/iusd-enrollment/pre-kindergarten-tk): PK replaced the district's former TK program; this supports combining IUSD PK and TK for the November 2025 attendance/meal comparison.
- [IUSD current calendar page](https://iciacademy.org/230365_2): currently retained 2025–26 calendar only.

Neither these school calendars nor the state minimum establish the duration-weighted expected attendance of each student. Calendar evidence is used only for explicit school-day sensitivity checks.
